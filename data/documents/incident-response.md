# Incident Response Guide

## Reporting an incident

Report production incidents through the incident management system.

The incident report should include:

- Short incident description
- Affected application
- Business impact
- Start time
- Current status

## Severity levels

Severity 1 means a critical production outage affecting a major business process.

Severity 2 means significant degradation affecting multiple users.

Severity 3 means a limited issue with an available workaround.

## Incident communication

The incident commander is responsible for coordinating communication and assigning investigation tasks.
